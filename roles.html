我故事里的人。 People in my stories.

每个世界都有自己的角色。记录他们的方式，就是记录我的方式。 Every world has its characters. The way I record them is the way I record myself.

CHAR.01

冬莲奶奶Grandma Donglian

家族记忆的主角The memory-keeper

她叫我夏娃。不识字，却算得准我每次回家的时间。她做的菜别的地方吃不到。《十年一味》里所有味道的起点。She called me Xiawa. She could not read, yet knew when I was coming home. Her cooking exists nowhere else. The origin of every taste in "A Taste of Ten Years".

CHAR.02

矶崎新 · 胡倩

大师工作室 · 第 01 期Studio Visits No.01

走进他们的工作室，我才明白空间如何说话。我的视频系列里第一个被记录的人。Walking into their studio taught me how space speaks. The first person recorded in my video series.

CHAR.03

Thomas Heatherwick

大师工作室 · 第 03 期Studio Visits No.03

一个相信建筑可以让人微笑的人。我记录他如何把小岛做成会动的故事。A man who believes buildings can make people smile. I recorded how he turns an island into a moving story.

CHAR.04

大师工作室 · 采访Studio Visits · Interview

西安的建筑学者，也是把历史讲成当下的人。我的城市研究里重要的对话者。An architectural scholar of Xi'an who turns history into the present. A key voice in my urban research.

CHAR.05

李响

超级理想 · 口述者Super Idea · The speaker

一个把观点说成白话的建筑师。"工作室就是卖猪肉。"我帮他把想法变成观点视频。An architect who talks like a plain speaker — "a studio is selling pork." I helped turn his thoughts into videos.
