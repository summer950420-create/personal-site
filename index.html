01 世界观Worldview WHAT NARRATIVE IS
叙事不是修饰，叙事是结构。
一栋楼立得住，因为承重墙诚实；一个故事立得住，因为内部结构清晰。
我的工作是翻译：把专业语言翻成公众语言，把私人记忆翻成公共记忆，
把一座城市的剖面、一个人的一生，翻成可以被看见的图层。 Narrative is not decoration — narrative is structure.
A building stands because its load-bearing walls are honest; a story stands because its inner structure is clear.
My work is translation: professional language into public language, private memory into shared memory,
a city's section or a person's life into layers that can be seen.
