你好，我是李夏。
建筑学出身，做过建筑媒体，现在做独立的内容工作。
这些年我做过的事只有一件：把复杂的东西，讲成听得懂的故事——
帮设计机构把思考变成万人关注的账号，
帮事务所把项目讲进国际奖项的短名单，
也给自己奶奶办过一场关于味道的展览。
如果你也有一件复杂的事，想被讲清楚，
我们聊聊。 Hello, I'm Summer.
Architecture-trained, media-born, now working independently.
For years I have been doing one thing: turning complex things into stories people can hear —
growing a design practice's account to ten thousand followers,
carrying projects into international award shortlists,
and curating an exhibition about my grandmother's cooking.
If you have something complex that deserves a story,
let's talk.
