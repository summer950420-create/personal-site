CH.01 · 世界观CH.01 · WORLDVIEW

叙事不是修饰，
叙事是结构。 Narrative is not decoration —
narrative is structure.

一栋楼立得住，因为承重墙诚实；一个故事立得住，因为内部结构清晰。我的工作是翻译：把专业语言翻成公众语言，把私人记忆翻成公共记忆，把一座城市的剖面、一个人的一生，翻成可以被看见的图层。 A building stands because its load-bearing walls are honest; a story stands because its inner structure is clear. My work is translation: professional language into public language, private memory into shared memory — a city's section, or a life, into layers that can be seen.
