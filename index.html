一场展览 · 李夏的世界An Exhibition · The World of Summer Li

把复杂价值，
变成公众叙事。 Turning complex value
into public narrative.

这里展出的不是作品，是我理解世界的方式。
我的工作是翻译：把专业语言翻成公众语言，把私人记忆翻成公共记忆，
把一座城市的剖面、一个人的一生，翻成可以被看见的图层。
点击墙上的每一幅展品，走进一段故事。 What hangs here is not my work — it is the way I read the world.
My work is translation: professional language into public language, private memory into shared memory,
a city's section, or a life, into layers that can be seen.
Click any piece on the wall to step into a story.
