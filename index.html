同时发起"在录 LOCI ARCHIVE"建筑师品牌赋能平台，与"量子回声"个人IP孵化服务。 Also founding LOCI ARCHIVE, a brand empowerment platform for architects, and QuantumEcho, a personal IP incubation service.
