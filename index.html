把复杂价值，变成公众叙事。Turning complex value into public narrative.

这里展出的不是作品，是我理解世界的方式。我的工作是翻译：把专业语言翻成公众语言，把私人记忆翻成公共记忆，把一座城市的剖面、一个人的一生，翻成可以被看见的图层。沿墙往下走，三块展板，三个故事。 What hangs here is not my work — it is the way I read the world. My work is translation: professional language into public language, private memory into shared memory, a city's section or a life into layers that can be seen. Walk along the wall — three panels, three stories.

NO.01 讲故事的人Storytellers

我走进别人的工作室，把他们的空间文化翻译成故事。I step into other people's studios and translate their spatial culture into stories.

FIG.01 — 大师工作室 · 第 01 期 · 矶崎新Studio Visits No.01 · Arata Isozaki

建筑事务所是城市里最安静的地方之一。那里的墙、桌子、模型和纸，都在说话。2026 年起，我扛着相机走进一间间工作室——矶崎新、Heatherwick、郭廖辉、goa——把空间如何说话记录下来。Architecture studios are among the quietest places in the city — yet their walls, tables, models and paper all speak. Since 2026 I have carried a camera into one studio after another — Isozaki, Heatherwick, Guo Liaohui, goa — recording how space speaks.

NO.02 我的故事My Stories

记忆如何成为公共叙事：一场关于味道的展览，两次穿过大陆的行走。How memory becomes narrative: an exhibition about taste, and two journeys across continents.

奶奶走后的第十年，我在老宅办了一场展览。五道菜、一桌采访、一盏花灯——把私人记忆翻译成全家人都能走进的公共叙事。美西大环线是另一回事：一条 66 号公路，一个夏天的家庭录像，把路变成共同的记忆。Ten years after my grandmother passed away, I held an exhibition in the old house — five dishes, a round of interviews, one paper lantern: private memory translated into a story the whole family could walk into. The US West loop was another kind of story: one summer, one family, a road that became shared memory.

NO.03 城市的剖面City Sections

我读城市的方法：切开它，看结构；分图层，看关系。My way of reading cities: cut them open to see structure, split into layers to see relations.

FIG.03 — 西安 · 城墙内Xi'an · Inside the Wall

城市研究 · 观察写作
FIVE SERIES · ONGOING

FIG.03 — 三学街田野调查 · 在地商店 · 色彩地理Field notes · local shops · colour geography

西安的城墙、云南的色彩、美西的路。我带着建筑学训练出来的一双眼睛行走，把城市读成剖面和图层——这既是我的研究方法，也是我的写作方法。观察有五个系列：建筑媒体、城市切片、工作室窥视、方法论、人话。The wall of Xi'an, the colours of Yunnan, the roads of the American West. I walk with a pair of eyes trained in architecture, reading cities as sections and layers — my way of research, and my way of writing. Five series: media watch, city slices, studio peeks, method, notes.
